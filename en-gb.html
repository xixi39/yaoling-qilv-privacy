Yokai Chess Journey Privacy Policy (Draft)

DRAFT — NOT YET IN EFFECT. OpenPaaS advertising has not been fully integrated. The actual advertising providers, categories of data, retention periods, storage locations and any cross-border transfers must be verified. Do not use this page as the final launch policy until it has been completed.

Operator: Gong Lei (龚磊)
Contact email: 4764708760@qq.com

This draft describes the planned handling of information in Yokai Chess Journey. It will be updated to reflect the services actually used.

Game saves

The game uses local storage provided by your device or host platform to keep progress, line-ups, settings and related game state so you can continue playing. Clearing local data, changing devices, uninstalling or clearing application data may remove your saves. Game account login and cloud saves are not currently planned.

Advertising and third-party services

The game plans to display ads using OpenPaaS advertising capabilities. Ad requests are handled by the host application and its actual advertising providers. The providers and SDKs, data categories, purposes, methods, retention periods and policy links must be verified and listed after integration. Depending on the integration, these services may process device and network information, advertising identifiers and ad interaction information for advertising, measurement or security; actual processing depends on the configuration and provider disclosures. The platform or host app may independently process information needed to run the game. Please also consult their privacy policies. Where consent is required by law, it must be obtained before processing; available ad and privacy settings depend on the actual integration.

Retention and security

Local saves normally remain in device or host-platform storage until you clear the relevant data. Advertising and host providers determine the retention period, location and security measures for their processing under their policies and applicable law. These details must be verified before launch. The operator will apply safeguards appropriate to the risks of information actually processed by the operator.

Your rights

You may clear local saves using your device, browser or host platform settings; this may permanently erase progress. You may contact the email above to request access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent concerning personal information. For information independently handled by third parties, use their own rights channels. Requests will be verified and handled in accordance with applicable law.

Children and minors

Minors should read this policy and use the game with a parent or guardian. Appropriate protections will be applied under applicable law and actual service requirements. Where information of children under 14 is processed, guardian consent and additional safeguards will be obtained or applied as required by applicable law.

Updates and contact

This policy will be updated when game features, advertising or information processing changes, with notice as required by applicable law. For questions, contact Gong Lei at the email above.

Hosting this policy

This policy page is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security when a GitHub Pages site is visited. GitHub processes this information as the hosting provider; see the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.
