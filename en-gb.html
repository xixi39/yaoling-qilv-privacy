Yokai Chess Journey Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026. This policy describes the game’s local saves and the use of advertising services.

Operator: Gong Lei
Contact email: 4764708760@qq.com

This policy explains how information is handled in Yokai Chess Journey. The game is single-player and uses local saves; advertising is provided through OpenPaaS.

Game saves

This is a single-player game. Progress, line-ups, settings and related game state are stored locally on the device or host platform and are not uploaded to the operator’s game-save server. Saves normally remain until the relevant local data is cleared. Clearing platform or browser data, changing devices, uninstalling or clearing application data may remove saves. The game currently does not use game account login or cloud saves.

Advertising and third-party services

The game uses OpenPaaS advertising capabilities provided by SUD to display ads. Ad requests are handled by the host application and its advertising services. Depending on the host and integration, these services may process device and network information, advertising identifiers and ad interaction information for advertising, measurement or security. The information actually processed, its recipients, retention periods and privacy choices depend on the host application and advertising services; please consult their notices and privacy settings, or contact the operator at the email above. The platform or host app may independently process information needed to run the game. Where consent is required by applicable law, the relevant consent flow applies before processing.

Retention and security

Local saves normally remain in device or host-platform storage until you clear the relevant data. Advertising and host services determine their own retention periods, locations and security measures under their privacy notices and applicable law. The operator uses safeguards appropriate to the risks of information actually processed by the operator.

Your rights

You may clear local saves using your device, browser or host platform settings; this may permanently erase progress. You may contact the email above to request access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent concerning personal information. For information independently handled by third parties, use their own rights channels. Requests will be verified and handled in accordance with applicable law.

Children and minors

Minors should read this policy and use the game with a parent or guardian. Appropriate protections will be applied under applicable law and actual service requirements. Where information of children under 14 is processed, guardian consent and additional safeguards will be obtained or applied as required by applicable law.

Updates and contact

This policy will be updated when game features, advertising or information processing changes, with notice as required by applicable law. For questions, contact Gong Lei at the email above.

Hosting this policy

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security when a GitHub Pages site is visited. GitHub processes this information as the hosting provider; see the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Use and sharing of information

The operator uses information only for the purposes described in this policy. Where a change of purpose or scope requires notice or consent, the operator will provide it as required by applicable law. The operator will not share, transfer or publicly disclose personal information except as permitted or required by law or with the consent required by law. Advertising and hosting providers may independently process information under their own privacy notices.
